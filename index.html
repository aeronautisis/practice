Our mission

To turn every drone detection over Singapore into a clear, personal instruction that gets people to safety in time.

The Aero Dome concept layers sensors, command and control, and interceptors over Singapore's skies. PHYLAX is its civil layer: the part that faces the public. When the dome detects hostile drones, PHYLAX converts that detection into a warning on the phones of people in the threatened area, telling them what is happening, where to go and how much time they have.

Each person is directed to the nearest shelter they can actually reach, measured by walking time from where they stand rather than distance on a map. If the threat will arrive before they can get there, PHYLAX tells them to take cover where they are. Once safe, people mark themselves as sheltered, giving civil defence responders a live picture of who may still need help.

Interceptors protect the sky, but people on the ground still have to act. Minutes of warning only save lives when everyone knows what to do with them. PHYLAX closes the gap between detection and action.

Phylax (φύλαξ) is the Ancient Greek word for a guardian or watchman.

Why it matters

  • Drone attacks leave little warning

    Low-cost drones can be launched in numbers, fly low and arrive within minutes. No air defence can promise to stop every one, so the public must be ready to act on the warning they get.

  • A siren tells you something is wrong, not where to go

    Singapore has built protection into homes, schools and MRT stations, and its public warning sirens cover the island. What people lack in the moment is a personal answer: which shelter, how far, and whether there is time.

  • Density raises the stakes

    About six million people live and work in one of the most densely populated countries in the world. Commuters, students, visitors and workers are often far from home when a warning comes, so alerts must be instant, specific and available in all four official languages.

How it works

  1. Detect

    From the Aero Dome

    Air defence sensors identify hostile drones and predict the area under threat.

  2. Warn

    PHYLAX

    Everyone inside the threatened area receives an alert with a countdown, in their chosen official language.

  3. Guide

    PHYLAX

    PHYLAX compares walking time with time to threat, then routes each person to a reachable shelter or tells them to take cover where they are.

  4. Account

    PHYLAX

    People mark themselves safe. Responders see shelter loads and the areas where people are still unaccounted for.

Part of the Aero Dome

An air defence umbrella is built to stop threats in the sky. PHYLAX looks after the people underneath it.

  • Sensors find and track the threat
  • Command and control decides the response
  • Interceptors defeat it in the air
  • PHYLAX gets people on the ground to safety
